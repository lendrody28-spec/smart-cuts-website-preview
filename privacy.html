ClipDen privacy

Privacy should have small, clear switches.

Customers and providers get the same granular approach: choose what is visible, what stays private, and what is shared only for a visit.

Default promise: addresses are hidden unless the owner actively opts in. A suburb or general area can be shown instead.

Share with care

Less is often more.

A client can send their profile or a target style to a provider for a visit without making every detail public. A provider can show service information without publishing their home address.
